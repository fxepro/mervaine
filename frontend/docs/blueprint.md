# **App Name**: Mervaine

## Core Features:

- Institutional Homepage: A high-authority landing page presenting Mervaine Holdings' mission of preservation and stewardship with clear navigation to core divisions.
- Divisional Interactive Hub: Dedicated interactive showcases for Mervaine Rights, Mervaine Archives, and Mervaine Intelligence divisions.
- Intelligence Platform Preview: An introductory display for the future rights intelligence database tracking ownership, status, and licensing opportunities.
- Case Study & Trust Governance: Modules highlighting recovered royalties and restored archives alongside compliance and chain-of-title verification standards.
- Multi-Channel Inquiry System: Specialized forms for institutional inquiries, estate representation, and rights owner submissions.
- AI Asset Classification Tool: A sophisticated tool that uses reasoning to analyze user asset descriptions and determine the appropriate Mervaine division and service path.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Burnt Terracotta (#C25B4E) to evoke a sense of heritage and established permanence.
- Secondary Color: Harvest Gold (#DAA520) for a bright, retro-institutional warmth.
- Background: Aged Parchment (#FDF9F3) providing a bright, clean, yet classic research-institution aesthetic.
- Accent: Forest Ink (#2F3E33) for typography and deep contrast, replacing standard black for a softer, retro feel.
- Headlines: 'Fraunces' (soft serif) for a bold, retro-editorial look. Body: 'Inter' for modern institutional precision.
- Thick-stroked, geometric icons inspired by mid-century graphic design and archival cataloging symbols.
- A structured, asymmetrical grid that mimics the layout of a premium academic journal or high-end estate dossier.
- Staccato transitions and page loads that feel like a physical card index or archival drawer being accessed.
